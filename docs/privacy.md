# Privacy

Cloudflare hosts this tutorial and receives the information your browser sends
to request a page, including your IP address. Cloudflare Web Analytics, when
enabled, counts visits by page and referring site without using analytics
cookies. [Cloudflare's privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/)

On lessons that show ads, Google and its advertising partners can receive the
page address, IP address and browser information. They can use cookies and
similar identifiers to serve and measure ads and, with your consent where
required, personalize them. [How Google uses information from partner sites](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)

Where the European consent message appears, you can accept, decline or manage
individual options. To change an earlier choice, reopen **Privacy and cookie
settings** on a lesson that shows the message. You can also manage personalized
advertising through [Google's My Ad Center](https://myadcenter.google.com/).
